# Membership Programs Overview (synthetic corpus document)

Member organizations join at tiered levels and receive advocacy updates, research briefings, and event access. Renewal outreach begins ninety days before a membership lapses, and account owners review engagement history before any outreach is sent.

The membership operations team maintains the system of record for member accounts. Draft communications that reference a specific member account are treated as member-confidential and require a human reviewer before they leave the building.

Engagement signals include event attendance, briefing downloads, and committee participation. Aggregated, de-identified engagement statistics are classified internal and may be used in staff briefings without account-level detail.
