# AI Acceptable Use Summary (synthetic corpus document)

Staff may use approved AI assistants for drafting, summarization, and research on public and internal information. Member-confidential material may be processed only by assistants that enforce human review before release, and restricted personal data is out of scope for assistants entirely.

Assistants must cite the internal sources they drew from. An uncited claim in AI-drafted material is treated the same as an unsourced claim in human-drafted material: it does not ship.

Every assistant runs behind a policy gate that records its decision and reasons. Blocked and quarantined requests are reviewed weekly to tune the rules, with the explicit goal of reducing friction for safe requests rather than adding steps to them.
